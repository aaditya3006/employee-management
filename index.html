Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding TA Journal maintenance, allowances, downloads, and official guidelines.

What is a TA Journal?

A TA Journal is a detailed record of official journeys, dates, routes, travel mode, timings, distance and allowance details used to prepare a travelling allowance claim.

What is a Railway TA Journal?

It organizes the journey and allowance information a railway employee may need for a travelling allowance record, including journey dates, stations, timings, mode and applicable rates.

Can railway employees maintain a TA Journal online?

Yes. An online TA Journal can help employees enter journeys, maintain monthly records, review calculations and generate a printable record.

What information is required in a Railway TA Journal?

Typical fields include employee and posting details, month, date, origin and destination stations, departure and arrival times, mode of travel, distance, allowance rate and journey purpose.

What is the difference between a TA Journal and a TA Bill?

The TA Journal is the detailed movement and journey record. The TA Bill is the claim submitted for payment, usually supported by the journal and required certifications.

Can I download my TA Journal?

RailSaarthi provides a printable journal preview that can be saved or printed as a PDF through the browser print workflow.

Can I calculate travel allowance using an online TA Journal?

The tool organizes rates and journey entries for calculation, but employees should verify eligibility, applicable rules and final amounts with their controlling office.

Is this an official Indian Railways system?

No. RailSaarthi is an independent digital workflow tool and is not an official Indian Railways website or authorized government submission system unless explicitly approved by the relevant authority.
